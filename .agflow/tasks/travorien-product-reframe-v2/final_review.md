# Final xhigh Product Review

Date: 2026-08-25  
Reviewer mode: xhigh target-customer audit  
Target customer: 35-year-old German traveler, experienced international driver,
first China visit, ten days, upper-mid budget, independent-travel preference.

## Customer questions

1. **Clear in ten seconds?** Yes. The first viewport says “Drive China Your Way,”
   names mountains, villages and coastlines, and begins with travel desire rather than
   rental configuration.
2. **Why continue?** Five visually distinct Great Drives, editorial guides and one
   natural-language entry offer useful paths before commerce.
3. **A newly imaginable China?** Yes. Yunnan, Western Sichuan, Guangxi, Hainan and
   Xinjiang are presented as complete driving ideas, not destination pins.
4. **Does the Advisor improve decisions?** Yes for the bounded V2 knowledge set. It
   compares road difficulty, altitude, pace, family fit and driving limits while
   retaining learned preferences. The first comparison is explicitly deterministic;
   bounded AI interpretation is used later in customization.
5. **Would a Signature Drive be saved or bought?** Yunnan is sufficiently complete to
   customize and enter demo commerce. The other four are honestly editorial until a
   route graph and inventory boundary exist.
6. **Does Driving in China lower the main barrier?** Yes. It shows source-bound,
   city-specific guidance, documents, next actions and explicit unknowns without
   calling an application path an approval.
7. **Is the car part of the journey?** Yes. Cars appear only after route selection and
   are ranked with distance, terrain, altitude, luggage and parking context.
8. **Are hotels road-trip-aware?** Yes. Parking type/distance, vehicle access,
   late-arrival suitability, old-town access and route convenience affect ranking.
9. **Is the difference from Trip.com + ChatGPT + Rentalcars visible?** Yes: structured
   China road products, driving enablement, journey-aware commerce and the continuing
   My Drive operating loop are all visible in the product.
10. **Why keep using it after departure?** My Drive keeps the structured journey,
    fuel, parking, stops, navigation notes and user-confirmed road responses together.

## Final verdict

**PASS WITH NON-BLOCKING ISSUES**

The local Consumer Experience V2 is ready for demo delivery. It is not ready for real
policy, supplier, payment or permit operations.

### Non-blocking follow-ups

- Broaden open-language Advisor evaluation before external trials while keeping Drive
  and Claim ID validation deterministic.
- Raise very small auxiliary labels and media credits after a dedicated accessibility
  typography pass.
- Add browser-level automation for the three entry journeys and readiness session
  handoff; the current three journeys, seven required prompts and 390px mobile menu were
  manually exercised in the in-app browser.

## Evidence

- Missing, consumed or malformed readiness handoff fails closed and cannot expose a
  Build CTA or self-drive materialization.
- The 7/10/12-day Yunnan variants produce graph-valid route plans with declared dates.
- A non-Golden planned Trip completes the generic road-closure Reality loop.
- `npm run harness:full`: Harness validation, 82/82 tests, strict TypeScript, ESLint
  with zero errors/warnings, and production build all pass on the final tree.
