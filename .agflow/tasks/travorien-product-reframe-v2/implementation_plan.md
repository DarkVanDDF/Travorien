# Product Reframe V2 Implementation Plan

## A — Product and knowledge layer

1. Add the smallest SignatureDrive, Guide, source provenance, and readiness contracts.
2. Build five structured drive products with explicit content-only versus transaction-
   ready status and unique primary media.
3. Add official-source policy rules and fail-closed readiness evaluation.

## B — Discovery-led consumer surface

1. Replace the transaction-first first viewport with the “Drive China Your Way” hero,
   one large advisory input, and inspiration prompts.
2. Add Great Drives of China, editorial discovery, Driving in China, the four-part
   product story, and a closing AI entry.
3. Add browsable drive, guide, and Driving in China pages with record-specific metadata.

## C — Travel Advisor and journey convergence

1. Ground drive comparison in compact SignatureDrive knowledge and validated IDs.
2. Support valuable comparison/explanation before missing-field collection.
3. Convert inspiration, blank chat, and readiness contexts into the same typed Yunnan
   planning seed; preserve the existing PlanningSession and commerce authorities.
4. Support context changes, including an honest content-only Hainan branch.

## D — Journey-aware commerce and Drive

1. Add route-fit language to vehicle recommendations without changing booking authority.
2. Add parking, vehicle access, late-arrival, old-town access, and route convenience to
   hotel data, ranking, and offer presentation.
3. Reframe the ready Trip and Reality workflow as consumer My Drive: today, route,
   departure, fuel, parking, arrival, stops, and clear road updates.

## E — Evidence and review

1. Add product integrity, advisor, readiness, journey ranking, entry-path, media, and
   Reality compatibility tests while preserving the 66-test baseline.
2. Update product, architecture, domain, AI, scenario, source, and decision documentation.
3. Complete browser walkthroughs for the three requested journeys and seven advisor prompts.
4. Run xhigh target-customer product review, remediate high-confidence findings, then run
   `npm run harness:full` and finalize reports.

## Completion condition

The product must visibly sell the freedom and discovery of driving China, use official
evidence for driving readiness, preserve deterministic commerce and Runtime authority,
and make the differentiation from a generic OTA plus chatbot understandable without
requiring a README.
