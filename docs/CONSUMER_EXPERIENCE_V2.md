# Travorien Consumer Experience V2

## Product position

Travorien is an **AI Self-Drive Travel Service / Road Trip Operating Platform for inbound travelers in China**.

- Consumer promise: **Drive China Your Way.**
- Emotional value: freedom and discovery.
- Editorial proposition: **See the China tour buses don't.**
- Journey pillars: **Discover → Plan & Book → Drive**.

The experience begins with a road worth taking. Vehicle, hotel and reservation choices appear only after the traveler has selected a journey direction.

## Consumer information architecture

```text
/
├── /road-trips
│   └── /road-trips/[slug]
├── /driving-in-china
├── /guides
│   └── /guides/[slug]
├── /plan
└── My Drive (after Trip materialization)
```

The top-level navigation is Explore, Road Trips, Driving in China, Guides and Plan with AI. My Drive is a consumer facade rendered only when a structured `Trip` exists.

## Product-layer authority

The road-trip knowledge layer is separate from the Commerce `Catalog`.

```text
SignatureDrive / Guide / KnowledgeClaim / DrivingReadinessRule
                         ↓
                bounded Travel Advisor
                         ↓
            PlanningSeed / JourneyCandidate
                         ↓ only with routeBinding
               deterministic RoutePlan
                         ↓
         VehicleOffer / HotelOffer / Booking
                         ↓
                         Trip
                         ↓
          existing Reality Runtime authority
```

Editorial content changes do not alter commerce or Runtime catalog fingerprints. Gemini may interpret words and explain supplied knowledge; it never owns readiness, route validity, inventory, booking or Trip mutation.

## Three entry journeys

### Inspiration-first

Home → Yunnan Signature Drive → ten-day editorial itinerary → Customize with AI → Yunnan `PlanningSeed` → validated RoutePlan → vehicle → optional hotel → My Drive.

### AI-first

Home or `/plan` → natural request → deterministic Signature Drive retrieval → compare Yunnan and Western Sichuan → select a direction → same `PlanningSeed` and planning engine.

### Readiness-first

Driving in China → deterministic city/licence assessment → official sources and unknowns → easy-first-drive recommendation → same Travel Advisor. The result crosses this same-tab boundary once through session storage; personal answers are not encoded in the URL. A missing or malformed result returns to the checker instead of opening self-drive commerce.

## Travel Advisor rules

- Retrieval uses the five structured Signature Drives.
- The first drive comparison is deterministic. Bounded server-side AI remains available inside customization; no Gemini-generated statement is presented as catalog or policy authority.
- Recommendations return only known Drive IDs and Knowledge Claim IDs.
- Unknown IDs are rejected.
- Policy eligibility is never generated by the advisor; it can only explain an assessment from the readiness engine.
- Content-ready routes remain non-transactional.
- A two-hour daily limit does not silently reshape a standard drive whose known longest stage exceeds it.
- “No hotels” remains a supported Trip path for the Yunnan demo.

## Driving readiness

The checker returns `ACTION_REQUIRED`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `NEEDS_INFORMATION` or `UNKNOWN` in the current scenarios. “Likely able to apply” never means approved to drive.

Verified source set, checked 25 August 2026:

- State Council, 2025 Guide to Working and Living in China.
- Beijing Municipal Government, German driving-licence FAQ, updated January 2026.
- Beijing overseas motor-vehicle licence service page.
- Shanghai temporary driving-permit guide, updated January 2026.
- PRC Road Traffic Safety Law.
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs traffic-safety guidance.

## Journey-aware commerce

Vehicle offers include structured recommendation reasons tied to distance, duration, elevation, luggage and parking preferences. Hotel data adds:

- parking type and distance;
- vehicle access;
- late-arrival suitability;
- route convenience;
- old-town access time.

Unknown parking is penalized and cannot masquerade as on-site parking. All availability, rankings, prices and suppliers remain `demo-mock`.

## My Drive

My Drive is a pure consumer presentation over the existing `Trip` and Reality Runtime. It shows a preview day, suggested departure, drive time/distance, fuel, parking, planned stops, navigation notes, arrival/stay context, journey days and road updates. The UI hides Event/Impact/CandidatePlan/Runtime terminology while confirmation still submits the existing revision- and fingerprint-bound decision envelope.

## Media

Signature Drive primary imagery is public-source Wikimedia Commons media with visible author and licence provenance. Each drive uses a unique primary asset. Style-reference hotel media remains explicitly labelled as not depicting the actual demo property.

## Current limitations

- No live suppliers, inventory, payments, permit filing, navigation, traffic, weather or deployment.
- Only Yunnan is transaction-ready in the local demo.
- Gemini requests preserve state on failure. The deterministic Advisor and all Trip/Reality paths remain functional without a reachable Gemini upstream.
- Policy data must be reverified before any production use.
